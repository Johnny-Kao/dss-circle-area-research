1. The inward projection of the legacy 20-point polygon used to choose the registered circle center.
2. The check requiring its vertices to stay inside the original S2 Cell.

The replacement computes circle A's center from its **true circle** radius and a fixed positive overlap of 0.5 m; B radius is 1 m, fully in the neighboring Cell. This is a genuine non-legacy cross-cell overlap search fixture.

## Decision

- **20-vertex check not necessary** for an ASTM-conformant positive search contract test.
- **20-vertex check or an equivalent diagnostic witness is necessary to claim every produced fixture reproduces the historic bug.** A cross-cell geometric overlap alone is insufficient (Stanford is concrete evidence).
- These are alternative objectives. Do not treat 3/4 strict diagnostic CI as completion of all-red/all-green guarantee, and do not count an old-Green result as proof that an old defect is fixed.
- No-20 candidate is still useful as a generic, future-oriented boundary search test. To strengthen historical diagnostic coverage without referencing the old algorithm, it would need a separately demonstrated boundary-position selection rule, or a finite combination of near-edge queries validated to find the old defect across representative Planning Areas.
